# 🧠 LifeOS

> **Your second brain for everyday life.**

LifeOS is an AI-powered personal productivity platform designed to help users organize, remember, and manage every aspect of their daily lives in one place. Instead of juggling multiple apps for notes, tasks, calendars, documents, budgeting, and reminders, LifeOS combines them into a single intelligent workspace.

The goal is simple:

**Capture everything. Find anything. Forget nothing.**

---

# ✨ Vision

Modern life is fragmented across dozens of apps.

* Notes in one app
* Tasks in another
* Calendar somewhere else
* Documents scattered across folders
* Photos in the gallery
* Ideas forgotten

LifeOS brings everything together and uses AI to understand relationships between your information, helping you stay organized without manual effort.
